# 1. Introduction to NotebookLM

NotebookLM is Google's AI-powered research and knowledge assistant.

Unlike traditional chatbots, NotebookLM works primarily on:
- YOUR uploaded documents
- YOUR knowledge sources
- YOUR research material

NotebookLM is designed for:
- research
- enterprise knowledge
- study material
- policy analysis
- reports
- document intelligence
- knowledge synthesis
